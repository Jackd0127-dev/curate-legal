Who we are

Local-first. Private by design.

Curate is built around a simple idea: your photo library should stay yours. The app works on your device with Apple Photos, without accounts, profiles, or a custom cloud backend.

  • Works with your existing Apple Photos library.
  • No sign-up or login required for the current app.
  • No custom backend storing your photo library.
  • You stay in control of Photos access and library changes.
